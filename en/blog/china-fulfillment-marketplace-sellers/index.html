Fulfillment in China for Marketplace Sellers: Packing, Labelling, Shipping

Fulfillment in China is all the work done on your goods between the supplier and shipping: receiving, inspection, storage, labelling, packing and kitting. For sellers on Russian marketplaces such as Wildberries or Ozon, it means the batch arrives ready to go to the marketplace warehouse. Labels, barcodes, individual packaging and even Chestny ZNAK marking codes can all be prepared in China, before international shipping.

25 November 2026·6 min read

Below we cover what marketplaces expect from a shipment, which marking rules apply to imports and what you can do while the goods are still in China. If you would like to discuss your product right away, send us a request.

What fulfillment in China means

Fulfillment in China starts once the supplier has shipped the goods. First, the batch goes to a fulfillment center in China. There it is received, counted and inspected, and if needed stored, labelled and packed. Then it is combined with other lots into one consignment and sent by the shipping method you chose.

Do not confuse it with a marketplace's own fulfillment. Under FBO the marketplace stores, picks, packs and delivers the goods to the buyer, while under FBS the seller does this1. Fulfillment in China happens earlier: it prepares the goods so that the marketplace warehouse in Russia will accept them.

The point is that mistakes can be fixed on the spot. Say units are missing, a product has no barcode or the packaging does not suit the marketplace. That is easier to sort out in China than after the cargo arrives.

What a marketplace expects from a shipment

Each marketplace has its own rules, and they change over time, so check them in your seller account before every new shipment. The idea at Wildberries and Ozon is the same, though: the warehouse must be able to identify every unit and every carton quickly.

A barcode label on every unit. According to MoySklad, incorrect labels are one of the main reasons Wildberries refuses to accept goods1. For FBO, Ozon asks for a readable barcode sticker of at least 40 × 25 mm on every unit, with brief product details in Russian2.

A barcode visible without unpacking. If a product sits in extra packaging, Ozon wants the same barcode and sticker repeated on the outside, even when the packaging is transparent2. Wildberries also asks for the barcode to be repeated on a separate sticker outside, so warehouse staff do not have to unwrap each item1.

Individual packaging for every unit. At Wildberries, a bag must be securely closed, sealed or zip-locked, and a box must have its joins secured with tape, stickers or shrink film1. Cling film is not acceptable as individual packaging1.

A separate label for each carton or pallet. Under FBO at Ozon, the shipment label belongs to the cargo unit, not to the product2. A label smaller than 120 × 75 mm cannot be read by the scanner. A4 is the recommended size2.

Labelled cartons. MoySklad advises writing the SKUs and quantities on each carton1. It makes packing easier for you and receiving easier for the warehouse.

Labelling in Russian

The marketplace looks at stickers, while the law looks at product labelling. Clothing and other light industry products are covered by Customs Union Technical Regulation TR CU 017/2011. Products for children and teenagers fall outside it3, so check their requirements separately. Under it, labelling must include the product name and the manufacturer's country, name and legal address. It must also show the size, the material composition, the single EAEU conformity mark and the date of manufacture3.

Labelling and information must be in Russian or in the state language of the EAEU country where the product is sold3. For imported products, the country, the manufacturer's name and its address may be given in Latin letters3. Labelling goes on the product, an attached label, a swing tag, the packaging or a leaflet3.

Other product categories have their own regulations. Check which requirements apply to your product before you order labels.

Chestny ZNAK codes on imported goods

Chestny ZNAK is Russia's mandatory product marking system. If your product is on the mandatory marking list, it needs a Chestny ZNAK code. The importer must order the codes and make sure they are applied4. For goods from outside the EAEU, including China, the codes must be applied before the goods are placed under a customs procedure4.

According to the Chestny ZNAK operator, imported products can be marked in three places4:

  1. At the foreign factory.
  2. At a foreign consolidation warehouse.
  3. At a customs warehouse in Russia.

The codes are listed in the customs declaration for marked goods4. We explain how shipping with a declaration differs from cargo service in our article on cargo vs full customs clearance.

At Wildberries, the code sticker must not cover the product label1.

What to do in China, before shipping

All of the steps below can be done at a warehouse in China. Then, after shipping and customs clearance, the batch only needs to be taken to the marketplace warehouse.

  1. Count and inspect the goods. Check the quantity against the order and catch defects before shipping. We explain how inspection works in our article on pre-shipment inspection in China.
  2. Put each unit into individual packaging that meets your marketplace's rules.
  3. Apply barcode labels. The barcode on the product must match the barcode in the product card and in the shipment2.
  4. Apply Chestny ZNAK codes if your product requires marking.
  5. Replace the supplier's packaging with yours if you sell under your own brand. We cover launching such a product in our article on private label manufacturing.
  6. Assemble sets and bundles if they are sold under one product card.
  7. Pack into cartons and label them so the warehouse can see the SKU and quantity.

What to send your fulfillment provider

To get the batch prepared without rework, send:

  1. A product list: SKUs, quantities, sizes and colours.
  2. The barcodes from your seller account for each SKU.
  3. The label text in Russian.
  4. Chestny ZNAK codes, if your product requires marking.
  5. Packaging requirements: the marketplace, the sales model, and your branded packaging if you have it.
  6. The contents of any sets.
  7. Where and how to ship the cargo.

How to choose a shipping method is covered in our article on air, road, rail and sea freight.

How FBL Group works

Your suppliers send the goods to our warehouse in China. On arrival, we receive them, check the quantity against the order and inspect the quality. The goods are photographed and filmed, and we send you a report.

After that, the goods stay in our storage, with records kept by product and batch. Barcodes, labels and Chestny ZNAK codes are applied here too. Packing follows the requirements of the marketplace or the buyer. We can also replace the supplier's packaging with yours and assemble orders and sets from your list. The lots are then combined into one consignment and handed over for shipping by the method you chose. Returns are received and handled under an agreed process.

You can order a single operation or the full cycle. You pay for the operations on your goods, with no mandatory service packages. Learn more on our Fulfillment page.

In short

  1. Fulfillment in China prepares goods before international shipping, while a marketplace's FBO works at its own warehouse.
  2. Marketplaces expect a barcode label on every unit, suitable packaging and labels on cartons or pallets.
  3. Under TR CU 017/2011, clothing and textiles, except products for children and teenagers, must be labelled in Russian. The manufacturer's details may be in Latin letters.
  4. Chestny ZNAK codes for imports are applied before customs clearance, including at a foreign consolidation warehouse.
  5. The more you do in China, the less rework you face after the cargo arrives.
