Cargo service or customs-cleared shipping

We do both. The right one depends on the product, the volume and the documents you need at the destination.

  • Cargo service

    A simple format: the carrier handles clearance, you receive the goods without separate paperwork.

  • Customs-cleared shipping

    Full customs clearance of the shipment.

  • Not sure which?

    Describe the cargo in your request and we will suggest the format and route.

FAQ

  • Where do you ship?

    Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Europe, the USA, Canada, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia. The list is not closed.

  • How much does shipping cost?

    It depends on weight, volume, product, route and format. Send cargo details in a request and we will quote.

  • Can you ship goods from my supplier?

    Yes. The supplier sends goods to our warehouse, then we prepare and ship them. See Fulfillment.

  • How do I track cargo?

    Enter the cargo number on the tracking page.

  • What if the goods are not bought yet?

    We find and buy them: see sourcing in China.

  • Will you check goods before shipping?

    Yes, at our warehouse or on site at the supplier: quality inspection.
